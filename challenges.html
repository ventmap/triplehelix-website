How it works

One cycle, five stages

Every challenge follows the same path, from a written brief to a published decision.

  1. Brief

    The owner names one problem, a decision date and a budget holder.

  2. Open call

    Six weeks of applications from research groups, student teams and startups.

  3. Shortlist

    Independent scoring. The owner confirms who proceeds.

  4. Pilot

    Eight to ten weeks on a fixed scope.

  5. Decision

    One of six outcomes, announced at GTW Baku.

Outcomes

Six possible endings

Every pilot ends in a decision, and every decision is published.

Scale

The owner rolls the solution out across its own operations.

Partner

Owner and solver agree a longer-term collaboration.

License

The owner licenses the solver's technology.

Spin out

The research becomes a new company.

Invest

An investor backs the team once the decision is published.

Stop

A clear no, backed by evidence, is a valid and useful result.

Safeguards

The owner gets an evaluation licence and an option to license. The university’s transfer office signs.

Two layers of confidentiality

A public one-page summary of the problem. Full detail only after an NDA.

Independent scoring

Interests disclosed

FAQ

Questions we expect

Who can submit a challenge?

Any company or organisation with a real problem, a named decision-maker and a named budget holder.

Who can apply to solve one?

Research groups, student teams and startups.

Who owns the intellectual property?

The solver keeps its IP. The owner receives an evaluation licence and an option to license. For research groups, the university’s transfer office signs.

Will my problem be made public?

Only a one-page summary. Full detail is shared after an NDA.

How are applications judged?

The challenge owner and an independent academic score them. TripleHelix does not decide alone.

What does it cost?

Terms for the first cycle are being finalised. Register your interest and we will send them before the call opens.

How is TripleHelix connected to Farabi.vc?

Both are part of the same ecosystem. Farabi.vc considers a winner only after the decision is published, and the link is stated openly.
