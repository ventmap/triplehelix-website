What We Do

Three services, one relationship

Practical support, from the first idea to a working partnership.

01

Innovation & business development

Build the capability to innovate: strategy, systems and programmes that work in daily operations.

  • Innovation strategy
  • Digital transformation
  • Business process improvement
  • Corporate innovation programmes
  • Innovation management systems

Who it’s for

Companies, state-owned enterprises and public bodies that want a structured approach to innovation and digital change.

How We Work

We start with one department or one pilot idea, agree the scope in writing and support delivery against international frameworks such as ISO 56002 and ISO 56005.

Expected outcome

A working innovation approach, a clear roadmap and a first pilot.

02

Science–industry cooperation

Connect research to the companies that can use it.

  • University–industry cooperation
  • Technology scouting and matching
  • Commercialisation of research results
  • Technology transfer
  • Joint R&D projects
  • Consortium building

Who it’s for

Universities, research institutes and businesses looking for technology or research partners.

How We Work

We scout technologies, match them with business needs and support the agreements, consortia and joint projects that follow.

Expected outcome

Joint R&D projects, technology transfer agreements and research results that reach the market.

03

Business relations & international cooperation

Open doors to the right partners, markets and investors.

  • B2B matchmaking
  • Business clubs and CEO networks
  • International business missions
  • Connecting with investors and partners
  • Participation in international projects

Who it’s for

Companies and institutions looking for new partners at home and abroad.

How We Work

We run B2B matchmaking, business clubs and international missions, drawing on our partner GTW's network.

Expected outcome

Qualified introductions, partnerships and access to international opportunities.
