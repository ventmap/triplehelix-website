The Idea

Where science, industry and government meet

Innovation grows from the cooperation of three actors, as the Triple Helix model of Etzkowitz and Leydesdorff describes. Azerbaijan has the strategy. What is missing is a structured bridge between them — and that is the gap TripleHelix fills.

Business & Industry

The enterprises and sectors that turn capability into commercial outcomes.

Science & Education

The universities and research institutions where knowledge originates.

Government

Public institutions that set policy, fund research and open markets.

Where the three meet, innovation happens.
