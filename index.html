Three gaps, one missing bridge

Azerbaijan's digital and innovation strategies set a clear national direction. What's missing is a consistent, structured mechanism connecting the institutions meant to deliver it.

Fragmented consulting

Innovation and transformation advisory is largely unstructured today, delivered by individual consultants rather than an integrated practice.

Weak systematic coordination

No consistent mechanism connects business, science, government, and technology as a matter of course — each collaboration starts from zero.

Limited international integration

B2B networking activity exists, but formats integrated with international standards and frameworks remain limited.

MISSION
Give Azerbaijani institutions practical support in adopting global management, technology, and innovation practices — and connect them to the right partners, markets, and opportunities.
VISION
To become the trusted bridge between business, science, government, and technology in Azerbaijan — and a recognized innovation and partnership center across the region.

Why we're called TripleHelix

Developed by Henry Etzkowitz and Loet Leydesdorff, the Triple Helix model describes how innovation accelerates fastest not inside any single institution, but in the overlap between three strands. In our corridor, government's role runs specifically through education and science policy — so we name our three strands the way they actually meet in the room.

Business & Industry

The enterprises and sectors that turn capability into commercial outcomes.

Education & Science

The universities and research institutions where knowledge originates.

Innovation

The capability their collaboration produces — not a fourth strand, but the result of the other two meeting.
